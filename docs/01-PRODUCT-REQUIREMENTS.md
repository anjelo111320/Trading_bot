# Product Requirements

## Users
- Individual traders.
- Quantitative researchers.
- Trading strategy developers.

## Main Features
1. Live market dashboard.
2. Multi-timeframe chart analysis.
3. Technical indicators.
4. Price-action detection.
5. News monitoring.
6. AI market analysis.
7. Trade recommendations.
8. Risk calculations.
9. Alerts.
10. Historical backtesting.
11. Paper trading.
12. Performance analytics.

## Recommendation Actions
- BUY
- SELL
- WAIT
- NO_TRADE

## Recommendation Fields
- Instrument.
- Direction.
- Entry type.
- Entry price or entry zone.
- Stop-loss.
- TP1, TP2 and TP3.
- Position size.
- Reward/risk.
- Evidence.
- Conflicting evidence.
- Invalidation conditions.
- Creation timestamp.
- Expiry timestamp.
- Data freshness.
- Current market price.
- Maximum account risk.
- Estimated fees.
- Estimated slippage.
- Technical explanation.
- Fundamental explanation.
- Confidence assessment, separated into setup quality, news alignment, data reliability and calibrated probability.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Reliable WebSocket updates.
- Secure credentials.
- Auditable recommendations.
- Low unnecessary AI usage.
- Responsive dashboard.
- Reproducible calculations.
